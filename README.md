# TileTales privacy policy

The privacy policy for **TileTales - Slide Your Time Away** (Android) by koidafish, as a static page for Vercel.

- `index.html`: the policy page (no build step).
- `vercel.json`: `/privacy` and `/privacy-policy` redirect to the policy.

## Deploy

In Vercel, **Add New → Project**, import this repository, keep the framework preset as **Other** with no build command, and deploy.
Use the deployment URL as the privacy policy link in Google Play Console (**App content → Privacy policy**) and in AdMob.

## Keep it accurate

Update the policy and its dates whenever the app changes what data it uses, for example when adding an ad network,
analytics, accounts or new permissions. The Play Console **Data safety** form must match it.
